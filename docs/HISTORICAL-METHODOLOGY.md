# Historical methodology

Targets 1930, 1965 and 2000 are content milestones, not evidence that models exist. Initial app offers present only. Historical requests explain coverage limits instead of swapping guessed coastlines.

Prefer dated National Archives, NLB, SLA and published survey/map records with cleared terms. Preserve map edition, scale, scan resolution and attribution. Georeference against stable control points; record transform, residuals and uncertainty envelope. Separate map publication date from feature date. Do not interpolate reclamation as a factual continuous coastline without evidence.

Each reconstruction needs evidence IDs, method, assumptions (including building heights), confidence, horizontal/vertical accuracy and author/reviewer. A documented footprint with inferred height is reconstructed geometry. Absence on a map does not prove an asset did not exist. Preserve unknown regions visually. Compare from the same world origin and camera with dates and confidence displayed. Never move historical geometry simply to make a transition attractive.

Release gate: at least one licensed georeferenced pair, residual report, uncertainty UI, source review and a same-camera transition test before enabling any historical year.
