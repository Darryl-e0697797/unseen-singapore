# Future scenarios

2050 is an exploration horizon, not a prediction or government plan. Future models require scenario_id, title, assumptions, author, evidence IDs, uncertainty, alternatives and date. ENVISIONED label persists in scene, narration and screenshots. Official proposals may be DOCUMENTED as proposals while imagined completed geometry stays ENVISIONED.

Candidate comparisons: coastal protection alternatives, demand/reuse pathways, land-use consequences. No numerical flood, hydraulic or structural output until a separately validated model and its input assumptions exist. Representative animation never receives a simulation label.

Future schema is established but no scenario geometry ships in the first slice. A scenario selector will preserve camera and spatial origin, display assumption differences, and prevent future assets leaking into present mode. Gate: reviewed assumptions and at least two alternatives, honest uncertainty and user-visible official-versus-independent distinction.
