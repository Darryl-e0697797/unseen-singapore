# Source provenance

Four persistent labels:
- DOCUMENTED: a claim or geometry directly supported by a reliable public source.
- RECONSTRUCTED: evidence plus explicit assumptions and confidence.
- SCHEMATIC: explanatory relations, no survey precision.
- ENVISIONED: a named future scenario, not an official forecast.

Every story shows sources beside its explanation. Every scene permanently shows geometry accuracy. Source UI distinguishes original prose from source ownership. No government logos or implied partnership.

## Acquisition gate
Before ingesting any dataset record publisher, canonical URL, publication/update date (null if unknown), access date, terms URL, licence, redistribution permission, attribution and intended use. Unknown/restricted redistribution blocks shipping source files or derived geometry until reviewed. A source can be cited for original factual summary without bundling its assets; this does not declare the underlying dataset open. Maintain source_ids on claims and asset_ids in source records, checked bidirectionally.

Reliability levels: primary, peer-reviewed, secondary. Prefer primary agency sources for project status. Conflicting sources remain visible with dated notes; do not silently select a convenient number. Review current infrastructure status before every public release and at least every 90 days. Access date is not publication date. Never infer an operational date from a scheduled date.

Initial research: PUB DTSS overview and LTA Downtown Line engineering page. PUB reports Phase 1 completed in 2008, and Phase 2 including Tuas WRP scheduled for commissioning in phases from 2027 as accessed 2026-09-14. The representative story avoids depicting the Phase 2 system as operating. No exact alignment or claimed clearance is imported. Underlying agency media are not redistributed. Terms not fully verified are explicitly pending in registry.

Editorial workflow: propose claim → primary evidence → licence gate for media → accuracy classification → independent factual review → publication. First local slice has not received independent engineering or agency review.

PUB terms were subsequently located at https://www.pub.gov.sg/termsofuse. Materials are proprietary with restricted reuse; the terms also address linking. Registry now marks restricted rather than unverified. Keep local development separate from publication review; obtain appropriate clearance before a public release. The DTSS page reports last updated 2026-03-03, recorded independently from its access date.

## Geographic surface sources

`content/geography/sources.json` registers the OneMap embedding documentation and bounded OSM extract, separately from the 30 engineering research sources. Required OneMap logo, contributor attribution and SLA link stay on the map. OSM attribution links to its copyright page; the derived CBD GeoJSON is downloadable under ODbL. Each building exposes tagged, estimated or unknown height basis. Checksums and feature counts are validated during builds. The live basemap is present-day context with no historical epoch implied; thematic project locations are approximate.
