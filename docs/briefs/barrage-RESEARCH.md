## Separate challenge pass

Conducted after initial source collection, by this same agent; not an independent engineer's certification.

- **Area:** 10,000 ha is catchment land, not lake area. Contractor wording blurs this; PUB controls the interpretation.
- **Dates:** 2008 public opening and 2010 freshwater commissioning are separate. Contractor “desalinated water” wording cannot establish a desalination plant at the Barrage.
- **Capacity:** 280 m³/s is the published seven-pump combined rating, not continuous flow. Type and individual motor data remain PUB single-source details even where totals corroborate.
- **Gate motion:** Lowering permits low-tide release. Do not depict a vertically lifted sluice gate as the main crest gate.
- **Flood risk:** Alleviation is part of a wider drainage system. No “Singapore cannot flood” or guaranteed flood-free catchment.
- **Future:** The 2025 completed study changes the older 2021 forecast. Retrofit concept is documented; final dimensions, delivery date and operational state are not established.
- **Construction confusion:** MCE contractor material about a 150 m temporary opening concerns another project. It must not be reused as Barrage construction evidence.
- **Cost conflict:** PUB's 2013 feature reports S$226m; BCA indexed award material reports S$250m. Scope differences unresolved: omit headline cost rather than silently choose.
- **Length conflict:** Public channel width, bridge length and rounded consultant marketing widths describe different things. Use only the PUB channel label and rounded gate dimensions in this revision.
- **Publication dates:** Paper issue is 2009; publisher online date is 2018. Brochure filename is not evidence of a publication date; obsolete visiting information is excluded.
- **Generated summaries:** A discovery service misdescribes “3-in-1” as three construction phases. Reject; official three benefits control.
