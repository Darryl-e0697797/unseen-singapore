## Separate verification pass: challenged interpretations

1. **First service date:** the 2022 LTA TBM article says 1986. The contemporary inauguration speech is dated 7 November 1987; the NSL page also says 1987. Use 1987. This is an identified source error, not something hidden by a passing validator.
2. **1982 versus construction start:** the 1982 speech records the policy decision. Do not describe that as the date all civil construction began. OCR renders a heading year incorrectly; the archive document identifier and content refer to 1982. Historical policy claims are limited to the decision, not construction commencement.
3. **Top-down versus bottom-up:** the link labelled diaphragm wall leads to a sheet whose body describes bottom-up. The second page of topdown_final contains actual panel-installation steps. Source locators distinguish the two. Do not blend the sequences.
4. **EPB versus slurry:** both occur in Singapore. The proposed EPB machine uses its own mechanism; it must not borrow slurry pipes to imply all machines work alike. The 2017 LTA-author paper and manufacturer description support the generic balance explanation. Manufacturer evidence is independent of the client account but not a second report on a particular Singapore site.
5. **Lining detail:** paper describes changing arrangements over time. No universal tunnel diameter, segment count, bolt pattern or gasket design is approved. The close-up remains schematic.
6. **Freezing applicability:** LTA describes different treatments for the two Marina Bay TEL tunnels. Freeze only the separate lower-tunnel construction case; do not freeze the entire MRT or retain ice in operation. Ground heave/thaw behaviour exists but no numerical animation is proposed.
7. **Old forecasts:** the April 2015 DTL2 interview forecasts early 2016; the DTL page records actual opening on 27 December 2015. Its site-construction account remains usable, its forecast does not.
8. **2026 currency:** CCL6 latest-updates text confirms passenger service on 12 July 2026 despite a residual “expected completion” tile. March 2026 JRL timing supersedes earlier 2027 targets. No confirmed TEL5/DTL3e opening was found; keep the dated target, not an operating claim.
9. **Depth superlatives:** the DTL page contains deepest-station/tunnel language in historical context. Avoid “Singapore’s deepest” in a current comparative claim without a fresh network-wide check.
10. **Map versus alignment:** the July 2026 system map is schematic topology. It cannot establish underground depth, curve geometry, foundation clearances or precise station footprints. The generic rail overview also retains broad legacy figures; omit a national exact length/count unless reconciled.

This was a separate challenge pass by the same assistant. No independent engineer or government reviewer has approved the interpretation. Claims explicitly marked single-source remain so.

## Unknowns and enforceable exclusions

- No complete as-built station/shaft/utility geometry or construction programme: use labelled teaching compositions; omit precise coordinates below ground.
- No station-specific soil logs, water levels, face-pressure settings or alarm thresholds: show qualitative relationships, not simulated safe/unsafe engineering limits.
- No verified individual construction yard, muck destination or logistics fleet: explain directions and interfaces only.
- No complete year-2000 geographic network snapshot: display contextual history; do not reuse the modern route map as historical truth.
- No final 2050 official rail plan: envisioned state only. New alignment detail would require evidence and scope review.
- Exact map anchor coordinates and permitted data terms must be resolved during map implementation before those features ship. They do not justify modelling underground alignments from approximate surface positions.
- Detailed fire, smoke extraction, evacuation and maintenance procedures are outside this build. Fit-out labels explain systems at a functional level.
