## Separate challenge pass

After the initial assembly, reread the Penta-Ocean award outline, Boskalis construction account and SMEC project status against the completed-project agency publications. This was a second interpretation pass by the same assistant, not independent peer review.

| Potential error                                                   | Resolution                                                                                                             |
| ----------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Older 810 ha versus about 800 ha                                  | Use the rounded completion description for current state; retain historical scope as dated. No inferred loss of land.  |
| Award drainage 29 km versus later 45 km                           | Different reported stages/scope; use later whole-system figure. Exact reconciliation unavailable.                      |
| Construction feature pond 100 ha versus completed 116 ha          | Use completed specification; do not combine areas or invent expansion timing.                                          |
| +0.5 m chart datum versus −1.2 m MSL                              | Different datums; no conversion without a verified benchmark relation.                                                 |
| 2018 award predicted April 2022; designer later forecast end-2024 | Superseded by the annual report and completion announcement. Do not label historical forecasts as achieved dates.      |
| All water supposedly travels through the pond                     | Infographic distinguishes cut-off outlets; keep catchment, internal drainage and saline seepage conceptually separate. |
| Two stations treated as identical pumps                           | Separate function and path; station count is not installed pump count.                                                 |
| Dike-first implies no earlier outlets                             | Dike enclosure milestone and local outlet-before-overlying-segment dependency can coexist.                             |
| Old page updated in 2026 must be current                          | Government interview retains construction-era language; use only relevant design testimony.                            |
| Rock/grass or reduced fill means no ecological impact             | Explicitly avoid that inference.                                                                                       |
| Overtopping design return period guarantees immunity              | Do not include the contractor's return-period figure in visitor claims without fuller design basis.                    |

Sources from the same joint release/JV are not independent corroboration. The ledger labels narrower single-source statements and qualifications. Unknown publication days remain null. Dates encoded in URLs are not silently accepted as publication dates.

## Open detail limits

No verified as-built GIS/drain alignments, pump internals/count/capacity, exact dike profile or cut-off-wall dimensions, operating setpoints, energy-use history, outlet-gate choreography, complete contractor programme, ground investigation logs, measured ecological outcomes or Tekong 2050 build schedule. Exclude these numerical/physical assertions. Full construction design cannot be reverse-engineered from publicity material.

Original teaching shapes are legitimate within these bounds. Ground layers may illustrate a mechanism with conspicuous schematic labels; no calculated settlement, calibrated flood map or operational advice. Public imagery remains a reference link unless reuse rights are established. Current basemap provenance remains separate from engineering evidence.
