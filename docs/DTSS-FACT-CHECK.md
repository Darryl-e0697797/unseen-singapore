# DTSS exhibit fact check — 14 September 2026

This audit covers the six DTSS component stops, the five construction stages, and the island collection graphic. The model is an original explanatory assembly combining historical Phase 1 construction and documented Phase 2 features. It is not an as-built survey or a verified construction programme.

| Exhibit claim | Finding and correction | Government evidence |
|---|---|---|

## Conflicting published totals

The current conveyance page presents 49 km of deep tunnels and 49 km of link sewers. The 2023 release annex divides the same 98 km total into 28 km domestic tunnel, 7 km industrial tunnel and 63 km link sewers. Record the difference; do not silently combine classifications into an invented breakdown. The exhibit retains the unambiguous Phase 1 48 km metric and avoids a new Phase 2 split.

## Visual conventions

The main 3D cutaway remains level for component inspection. Gravity is explained in a separate adjustable longitudinal diagram with a level reference, downhill arrows, and an influent pump at the treatment end. Slider values are drawing units, not gradient percentages. Animation speed is decorative and does not predict hydraulic capacity. Gate isolation is a second, separately labelled mechanism diagram. Both respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Network presentation follow-up

The plan selector now displays PUB’s unmodified current overview illustration from https://www.pub.gov.sg/-/media/PUB/DTSS/DTSS-Map-(1).webp (linked from the official DTSS overview page), with source attribution and zoom. This restores the visible island-wide relationship without inventing georeferenced routes. The official image itself is an overview, includes future facilities/decommissioning context, and does not enumerate every property connection. The two neighbouring model shafts are explicitly labelled as an editorial comparison of construction access and gate isolation, not a verified pair or a rule that these functions always require separate adjacent shafts.

### Integrated overlay supersedes the image viewer

The next iteration removes the floating image and draws original approximate corridors directly on OneMap, alongside surface-landmark icons. `dtss-map-layer.tsx` documents the source and the manually chosen coordinates. These are interpretive regional paths, not a digitisation, surveyed alignment, complete sewer network, or verified facility footprint. Phase colours identify programme context, not current commissioning of every segment. Link-sewer branches remain omitted rather than invented. The UI retains a link to PUB’s original illustration for comparison. Landmark markers do not imply a sewer connection or an alignment beneath them.
