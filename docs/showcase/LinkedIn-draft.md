What holds Singapore together?

UNSEEN Singapore lets you move from an island map into the engineering behind the city: deep sewers, port construction, MRT tunnelling, Marina Barrage and Pulau Tekong's polder.

I built this with GPT-6 Astra as a hands-on exploration of research, software development and 3D authoring. I set the direction, challenged the explanations and approved each detailed exhibit. Astra helped research public sources, develop Blender authoring scripts, build the interactions, test the app and prepare this showcase.

The result includes five detailed construction journeys and four simplified exhibits, with numbered components, interactive mechanisms and source-linked explanations.

The models are teaching illustrations, not surveyed engineering drawings. The in-app guide is authored content; it is not a live Astra service.
